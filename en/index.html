KLANKA

Build the mechanical arm piece by piece, then switch it on. Clank!

Klanka is a logic puzzle about building machines. Each level gives you a tray of counted pieces (straights, joints and the claw), and you must use all of them to get the arm from its base to the target without hitting anything. The obvious path is never enough: you have to find the right detour.

BRINEGAST

A Victorian port city, always at night and drowned in fog. Rumors of cults and monsters hide very human crimes. Silas Vane, a former hunter, follows the trail of the apprentice he once trained.

What to expect

  • Investigation: clues, documents and a map that reveals itself bit by bit.
  • The lantern: its light shows what is hidden, but it also draws attention.
  • Tactical combat against the creatures of the fog, with weapons that open new paths.
  • Isometric pixel art, played with the phone in landscape.
